Working with me

my profiles

In practice

My DISC profile (Discflow) is Steadiness, leaning Compliance. In plain terms:

  • I follow through. If I say I’ll do it, it gets done, and checked.
  • I think before I answer. If you need a decision now, say so; otherwise I’ll come back with a considered one, usually within a day.
  • Clear briefs get my best work. Tell me what good looks like. If priorities change, a one-line heads-up helps.
  • I’m quiet in big groups. Ask what I think, or message me after; I usually have a view.
  • Tell me directly when you disagree. I’d rather hear it than guess.
What helps, what doesn’t

SCARF is a short model of five things people watch for at work: status, certainty, autonomy, relatedness and fairness. Mine comes out strongest on certainty, then autonomy. In plain terms:

  • Give me the plan early. A date, a deadline and what good looks like, and I’ll run with it. Changes sprung on me at the last minute are the one thing that throws me.
  • Change I choose is fine; change that lands on me isn’t. If something has to move, tell me why and when, and I’ll adjust.
  • Give me the problem, not the steps. Tell me the outcome you need and leave the how to me. I’ll check in if I’m unsure.
  • I’ll ask questions up front. That’s me pinning down what you expect, not doubting you.
  • I’m not chasing credit or titles. A clear brief and room to work are what I’m after.
  • Happy working alone or alongside you. Either way, I like to know how a decision was made, even when it wasn’t mine.

SCARF® is a model from the NeuroLeadership Institute. Self-assessed, October 2026.

my side of the deal

Notes to myself, kept in public

My profiles say what I need from others. These are what I try to give back. If I slip on one, tell me.

  • I set the goal and the deadline, then ask how you want to approach it.
  • Before I say no to an idea, I ask one question about it first.
  • I name who did the work, in writing.
  • If a plan might change, I ask early when it will be final, rather than react late.
  • I ask whether you want the steps or just the goal. Not everyone wants the room I do.
