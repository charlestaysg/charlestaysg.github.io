Four things to build early

Money, meaning, people and health in later life. What the evidence says, and what I’m starting now.

the short version

  • Retirement planning usually means one number: how much money. I wanted to check what else decides how the later years go, so I read the public research.
  • Four things kept coming up: a monthly income, something worth getting up for, people you’re close to, and health. The last three also decide how far the money goes.
  • None of them can be built in a hurry at 65. So this page is what I’m starting on now, and where each claim comes from.

the four

  • 1
    money

    A monthly income, not just a total

    found
    CPF LIFE pays a monthly income for life from 65. The Standard plan pays the same amount every month. The Escalating plan rises 2% a year but starts lower. A home can be worth a lot and still pay nothing each month.
    doing now
    Planning in monthly income, and checking the years before the first payout.
    learned
    Count the monthly cash, not the net worth.
  • 2
    meaning

    A reason to get up

    found
    Mostly my own experience. In my year out in 2025, the hard part wasn’t the money. It was a week with nothing in it that someone else had planned. Going back to class fixed that.
    doing now
    Keeping a few projects that run on my own schedule: songs, videos and this site. Giving time, not only money.
    learned
    Practise planning your own week before you have to.
  • 3
    people

    Connected, not just surrounded

    found
    The US Surgeon General’s 2023 advisory puts the mortality risk of social disconnection on par with smoking up to 15 cigarettes a day. In Singapore, a Duke-NUS study found that lonely 60-year-olds can expect three to five fewer years free of limits on daily activities. About one in three older Singaporeans say they feel lonely.
    doing now
    Turning a few work contacts into friends while I still see them every week. Keeping up with the apps my circles use. Building more than one circle.
    learned
    Friendships take years. Start them before you need them.
  • 4
    health

    The last decade costs the most

    found
    Singapore leads the world on healthy life expectancy, yet the average resident still spends about 11 years in poor health: about 12 for women, 10 for men. The national guidelines suggest 150 to 300 minutes of moderate activity a week, strength work on two days, and balance training for those 65 and up.
    doing now
    Walking, with other people where I can, so it counts for people as well.
    learned
    A habit is easier to start before the first injury.
