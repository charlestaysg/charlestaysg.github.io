Microduck on video

A small walking robot you teach new moves. I haven’t tried one, so these are other people’s videos.

from the makers

Microduck is 25 cm tall, has 15 motors, a camera and a beak that picks things up, and comes with seven moves you can retrain yourself. More on the Pollen Robotics page. Why it’s on my list: spotted, not tried yet.
