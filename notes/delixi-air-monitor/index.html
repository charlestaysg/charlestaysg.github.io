Reading the DELIXI air monitor

An English guide to the DLX-FTJ0506, whose manual is only in Chinese. What each number means, when to worry, and what to do.

the short version

Unofficial. I wrote this from the printed manual and my own unit. DELIXI did not make, check or endorse it.

  • Worry numbers: formaldehyde at 0.1 or more, TVOC at 0.6 or more, PM2.5 above 75.
  • The digit colours mean nothing. PM2.5 shows in red even at a clean reading of 4. Read the number, or the row of faces.
  • One high reading proves little. Perfume, alcohol wipes, smoke and cooking can all send it up falsely. Remove the cause, air the room and read again.
  • Daily readings and the proper test are different things. The manual's test needs a room shut for 12 hours with the air-con off.
  • Give it 180 seconds after switching on, and 5 to 10 minutes after moving to a new room.

These limits are averages over the times shown, so a single glance at the screen is not a direct comparison. All figures come from RESET's published comparison tables, not the standards themselves. SS 554 covers air-conditioned non-residential buildings. RESET's numbers appear to be from the 2021 edition, and SS 554:2026 has since replaced it; I could not see the new limits. A TVOC figure also depends on the reference gas used, so numbers from different systems may not line up.

a fair test

First time out of the box

Switch it on somewhere well ventilated for 30 to 60 minutes. This wakes the sensors up. Ignore every number during this time.

The manual's proper test

  1. Keep the room shut for 12 hours at normal temperature and humidity.
  2. Turn off anything that moves air: purifier, air-con, fan, humidifier.
  3. Put the unit in the middle of the room, 80 to 150 cm off the floor.
  4. Don't breathe on the vents or block them.
  5. Keep perfume, paint, cigarettes and alcohol away.
  6. Moving to another room? Wait 5 to 10 minutes for the numbers to settle.

What makes it read falsely high

The manual warns that these can send readings shooting up:

  • Breweries and other places thick with alcohol vapour
  • Make-up rooms and anywhere with strong alcohol-based scents
  • Heavy perfume, toner or skincare on the person testing
  • Alcohol-based cleaners being used nearby
  • Smoking rooms, and kitchens during stir-frying
  • Smoking or exhaling straight at the unit
  • Holding it over a stirred tin of paint or anything strong-smelling

Temperature, humidity, alcohol, smoke and sharp smells all affect the formaldehyde reading. Small ups and downs between times of day or parts of a room are normal.

what to do

These are my suggestions, not the manual's.

If you seeCheck firstThen
TVOC 0.6 or morePerfume, wipes, cooking, new paint or furniture nearbyOpen windows for 30 minutes and read again. If it stays high with the obvious causes gone, hunt for a hidden source.
Formaldehyde 0.1 or moreAny item on the false-high list aboveRun the 12-hour test on two different days. If both are high, air the room hard and pay for a lab test before spending on any treatment.
Formaldehyde above 0.5, or sore eyes or throatDon't stop to checkLeave the room and open it up. See a doctor if symptoms carry on.
PM2.5 above 35Cooking, incense, candles, haze outsideCooker hood during cooking. In haze, shut windows and run a HEPA purifier.
Humidity above 60%Damp walls, wet laundry, windows open in the rainAir-con dry mode or a dehumidifier, more so in rooms with cupboards and books.
A sudden spike that fadesSomeone walked past in perfume, or sprayed somethingUsually nothing. Only sustained readings matter.

Habits that make it more useful

  • Watch the trend. Same spot, same time of day. A change matters more than one exact figure.
  • Place it away from the stove, windows and a purifier's airflow, at about breathing height.
  • Zero TVOC only in clean air, after airing the room well. Zeroing in stale air sets a false baseline, and later readings will come out too low.
  • Use the 12-hour test sparingly, say after renovation or new furniture. In Singapore, a shut room with the air-con off soon stops being "normal" conditions, so test in the cooler hours.
  • Match the filter to the problem. HEPA catches particles. It does nothing for gases, which need activated carbon. Notes on how I chose a purifier.
  • Don't use it as the final word. Home sensors lose sensitivity with age, and TVOC sensors in particular react to alcohol more than to many other gases. Before paying for treatment or arguing with a contractor, get a proper lab test.
  • Check the clock if the date looks wrong. It only matters if you log your readings.

every term

On the screen

Formaldehyde甲醛 · HCHO
A colourless, sharp-smelling gas. Pressed-wood furniture, glues, some paints and cigarette smoke give it off.
Formaldehyde average甲醛均值
A running average of the formaldehyde reading. Steadier than the live figure. The averaging period isn't stated.
PM2.5 averagePM2.5 均值
Listed in the manual's features and drawn on its cover, but not visible on my unit's main screen.
TVOCTotal volatile organic compounds
One combined signal for many carbon-based gases that evaporate from paint, cleaners, perfume, cooking and more. It cannot tell you which gas it is seeing.
PMParticulate matter
Solid or liquid specks floating in air: dust, smoke, soot, pollen.
PM1.0
Particles up to 1 micrometre across. A human hair is roughly 70 micrometres.
PM2.5
Particles up to 2.5 micrometres. Small enough to reach deep into the lungs. The main haze measure.
PM10
Particles up to 10 micrometres. Includes all of PM2.5 and PM1.0.
Temperature温度
Air temperature at the unit, in degrees Celsius.
Relative humidity湿度
How much water vapour the air holds, as a percentage of the most it could hold at that temperature.
AQIAir quality index · 空气综合质量
Here, the unit's overall rating shown as four faces. It is not the same scale as Singapore's PSI or the US AQI number.
mg/m³Milligrams per cubic metre
Mass of gas in one cubic metre of air. 1 mg = 1,000 µg, so 0.328 mg/m³ equals 328 µg/m³.
µg/m³Printed "ug/m³" on the screen
Micrograms per cubic metre. A thousand times smaller than mg/m³.

Specification words

Measuring range测量范围
The lowest to highest value the unit can report.
Resolution分辨率
The smallest step on the display. It says nothing about how correct the value is.
Accuracy测量精度
How far the reading may be from the true value, written with ±.
Measuring principle测量原理
The physical method the sensor uses.
Electrochemical电化学 · formaldehyde sensor
The gas reacts at an electrode and produces a tiny current that rises with the amount of gas.
Semiconductor半导体 · TVOC sensor
Gases change the electrical resistance of a heated metal-oxide layer. It reacts to many gases, and alcohol strongly. Better for spotting changes than for exact figures.
Laser scattering激光散射 · PM sensors
A laser shines through the air. Particles scatter the light, and the sensor turns that into an estimated mass.
18650 lithium cell18650 锂电池
A standard cylindrical rechargeable cell, 18 mm wide and 65 mm long.
3.7V / 1200mAh
The cell's voltage and its capacity. Good for 6 to 8 hours here.
DC5V, 5V/1A
The power it needs: 5 volts direct current, up to 1 amp. Any ordinary USB charger.
LCD
Liquid crystal display, the screen type.
Q/LDL021-2021执行标准 · standard applied
The product standard printed on the manual. The "Q/" prefix marks a company's own standard in China, so it is not an air-quality limit.

The pass slip and the warranty card in the box.

what the manual leaves out
