What this does not tell you

  • Loading credit is not a verdict. I have barely used some of these.
  • Some of these cost more than they look. Several add Singapore GST on top of the credit, and unused credit may expire. Check each provider’s terms.
  • Your text goes to each provider’s servers. I keep private or work material on a model that runs on my own computer.
  • Names and prices change. This list will go out of date.

Checked 18 Oct 2026

When Claude is down I want a backup that doesn’t depend on Anthropic. Ollama’s cloud lists 20 open models. I sorted them by the job they do and what they cost, and put the Claude model of the same tier next to each group.

This is a match on role and price. It is not proof that any of them is as good as Claude.

Ollama prices are from its cloud list. Claude prices are OpenRouter’s list prices. Both as of 18 Oct 2026, and both change.

What this does not tell you

  • Price is not quality. A cheaper model can be weaker on your work.
  • “Close to Claude” is the maker’s claim. It compares with an older Claude. I haven’t checked it.
  • Most of these need a paid plan. When I tried the free Ollama plan, only the cloud Gemma 4 ran. The others asked for the Pro plan or bought credits.
  • Cloud means your text goes to servers. Ollama says it hosts the models mostly in the United States, uses partner data centres run by NVIDIA cloud providers, and does not log or train on prompts. That is Ollama’s own statement. I keep private or work material on a model that runs on my own computer.
  • You may not need Ollama’s cloud at all. Kimi K3, DeepSeek V4 Pro and GLM-5.3 are also sold through other services such as OpenRouter.

A fair test

Give three of them the same real task you would give Claude and read the answers side by side. Judge on your own work, not on a leaderboard. I’ll add my results here when I run it.
