Not showing as an SME?

Your company may be small enough to get the higher training subsidy and still not be on record as an SME. Fixing it takes about five minutes. Do it before you send staff for training.

the short version

  • For staff training, your SME status is worked out from government data. You don’t normally apply for it.
  • If that data is wrong or missing, your company is funded as a non-SME and pays more per course.
  • You can ask for a review online. A correction only counts for claims made after it is approved. Courses already paid for stay as they are.
  • The status can refresh at the end of each calendar year, so look again every January.

how to fix it

Checked 20 Oct 2026 against the official guide (last updated Sep 2026)

  • 1
    check

    Make sure you qualify

    rule
    Registered or incorporated in Singapore, and either 200 staff or fewer, or annual sales of S$100 million or less.
    also counts
    Sole proprietors, charities, societies and other groups not registered with ACRA.
    doesn’t
    Ministries, statutory boards and other government agencies.
  • 2
    access

    Get the right Corppass role

    who
    Your company’s Corppass administrator. If that isn’t you, ask them.
    what
    At corppass.gov.sg, under the Skills and Workforce Development Agency (SWDA), assign the role EPJS ENTERPRISE USER to the person who handles training claims.
    tip
    Do this first. Without the role, you can’t log in to apply.
  • 3
    prepare

    Have three numbers ready

    headcount
    Everyone employed under a contract of service, of any nationality.
    turnover
    Annual sales, as shown in your income statement.
    ownership
    The form asks for it. The criteria in the guide don’t mention it, so if you’re unsure what to choose, ask SWDA.
    files
    Supporting documents in PDF, JPG, PNG or GIF.
  • 4
    apply

    Submit and keep an eye on it

    where
    OurWorkforce e-services, then “Small Medium Enterprise (SME) Status Application”. Log in, open the SME Status tab and press “+”.
    after
    The same tab shows each application as Pending, Approved or Rejected.
    then
    Enrol staff once it says Approved.

Questions the guide doesn’t answer, like why a company shows no status at all, go to SWDA: 6669 7932 (Mon–Fri, 8.30am–5.30pm) or the SWDA service portal. Corppass questions go to the Corppass helpdesk: 6335 3530 (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm).
