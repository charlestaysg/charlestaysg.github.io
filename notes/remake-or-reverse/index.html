Remake or reverse?

ChatCut remakes a clip with new people or a new look. HyperFrames rebuilds a video as a project you can edit. Two different jobs.

the short version

  • ChatCut’s remake keeps a clip’s motion, camera moves and shot timing, and draws it again with new people, products, clothes, a new place or a new look. You get back a new video clip.
  • HyperFrames’ Reverse watches a video and rebuilds every layer (text, graphics, layout and timing) as a HyperFrames project. You get back a project you can change line by line and render again.
  • I use ChatCut and HyperFrames for my videos, but I haven’t used either of these two features on a real project yet. This page comes from each tool’s own description, checked on 5 Nov 2026.

where it runsGenerated in ChatCut’s cloud with Seedance 2.5. Motion-transfer reads the motion on your own computer firstBuilt on your computer by an AI agent with the HyperFrames skills. Renders in HyperFrames’ cloud by default, or on your computer. HeyGen makes the re-shot people

the two remake modes

object-swap
Changes only the people, products or clothes you name. Everything else stays, including other people and text on screen. It can keep a little of the original face.
motion-transfer
Draws the whole frame again in a new place or style, keeping the motion and camera. Recast people match their photos better, but large text burned into the video comes out garbled.

use this when

ChatCut
You want to change who or what is in the footage. Recast a role play, put your own product in, or restyle a scene.
HyperFrames
You want an editable copy of a video’s design and motion. Remake an explainer you like with your own words, colours, branding or data, or get back a project for an old video whose source files are lost.
neither
The video belongs to someone else and you don’t have the rights to it.

things I’d check

  • Your rights to the video. Use both on your own videos, or ones you have the rights to. Closely rebuilding someone else’s video can be copying.
  • The cost before it starts. In ChatCut’s own chat, a card shows the settings and the cost before anything runs. If an AI agent starts the remake from outside the app, the credits go at once, so ask it for the length and cost first.
  • Very different shapes. Both ChatCut modes keep the source’s poses, outlines and sizes. Swap a small bottle for a big box and it can look wrong.
  • The people in the video. Reverse re-shoots everyone on screen with HeyGen, from the original clip and voice. Check the people in it are happy with that, or ask for them to be cut from the original instead.
