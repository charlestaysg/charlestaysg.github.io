PMETs: retrenched vs open roles

Professionals, managers, executives and technicians. Retrenched is the total for 2025 Q3 to 2026 Q2; open roles are vacancies at the end of 2026 Q2. One is a year's total and the other a count on one day, so compare them side by side rather than as a ratio. A vacancy is not necessarily a fit for someone retrenched.
