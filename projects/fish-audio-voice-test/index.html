what I heard

  • my pick

    Drama 3: very good. The excitement sounds real, the laugh lands, and it takes its time. S2.1 Pro was good. The free version of the same model was decent.

  • brackets

    The direction in brackets changes the delivery a lot. None of the voices read the brackets out loud, which I checked by running every clip back through speech-to-text.

  • singlish

    The speech-to-text check stumbled on “leh”, “lah” and “not bad ah”. That says more about the checker than the voices. For local speech, trust your ears over a transcript.

  • a surprise

    I sent one request with a made-up model name. It still came back with audio, no error. So a clip that plays is no proof of which model made it. Listen to two side by side before you trust a setting.

  • two twins

    S2 Pro and S2.1 Pro came back the same length to the hundredth of a second. They may be the same model behind two names now.

run your own test

  • 1 · one line

    Pick a line you’d really use, with a feeling in it. A flat sentence makes every model sound the same.

  • 2 · one change

    Change one thing at a time: the model, then the voice, then the mood. Keep everything else the same.

  • 3 · listen blind

    Rename the files so you can’t see which is which, then rank them. Your favourite may not be the newest.

  • 4 · check rights

    Library voices belong to the people who made them. Personal tests are fine. For work or paid content, read the terms or use a voice you own.
