why I made it

In May 2025 I went back to class as a mid-career learner. I joined the first intake of a three-month SkillsFuture Career Transition Programme (SCTP) on automation and AI: six modules, most of them in one classroom on Level 4 of Block W6.

On day one I clicked the wrong thing twice in Power Query and nodded as if I understood. Then came Power BI, KNIME, machine learning without code, prompt engineering, and a Telegram chatbot that broke, and broke again, before it finally answered me.

What got me through was people. Lecturers stayed back after class to show me one more time. The admin team kept the paperwork moving so I could keep learning. My workgroup laughed through the hard parts with me.

I use what I learned every week now: dashboards that refresh before I reach my desk, forms that find their own way to sign-off, and at night the songs and films on this site.

If you’re standing at that door now, feeling worn, or too late, or out of date: you’re not discarded, and you won’t be alone. There’s a room of people learning, and someone will show you one more time.

how it was made

The song follows the year in order: lost on day one, helped by people, a role on the same campus, then a word to whoever comes next. Every lecturer is named in the credits, not in the lyrics.

the plan
Claude: the story shape, the lyrics with me, the course and trainer list from my own course files, and the checks
the song
Suno (v6): starts with piano and a quiet voice, builds to a full band and a key lift in the last chorus
the pictures
Google Flow (Nano Banana Pro): one character sheet so the learner looks the same in every shot, then 12 storybook scenes; the W6 opening is a watercolour of a campus view from RP360
the video
a short Python script with ffmpeg: slow camera moves, cross-fades, lyrics timed to the singing with Whisper on my Mac
