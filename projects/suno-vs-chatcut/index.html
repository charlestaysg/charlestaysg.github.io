From one test on 31 Oct 2026. Suno ran on its free model (v6-mini); its paid plans use newer models. One take each, so luck plays a part.

what I heard

  • my take

    Suno sings differently from ChatCut, and I like it. ChatCut is also good. I’ll keep both and pick by the feel a song needs.

  • fair test

    ChatCut’s file came out about 3 dB louder. Louder tends to sound better, so I levelled both before putting them side by side.
