why this isn’t slop

Anyone can type a prompt and get a song back in a minute. I didn’t start with a prompt. I started with a year: a new home, a retrenchment, going back to class, solo trips, a camera, and music finding its way back into my days. I asked an AI search engine about all of it, in plain words, as it happened. Those questions are dated. Reading them back, they told my year better than a diary would have.

AI sang it. I lived it. Every line below happened, and you can see the question that sits behind it.

the choices along the way

  • 1 · read back

    Pulled all 5,301 of my 2025 questions, with dates, and read them month by month.

  • 2 · words

    Drafted the lyrics from what happened, with Claude, then checked each line against the dated questions. Took one personal detail out before it went public.

  • 3 · first take

    Made a version with English verses and a Mandarin chorus. Listened, and scrapped it.

  • 4 · duet

    Rewrote it as a duet in English. I sing my side, and a second voice answers me as “you”, like someone walking beside me through the year.

  • 5 · timing

    Transcribed the finished song word by word and matched it to the lyrics line by line. Fixed three lines by hand where the transcript drifted.

  • 6 · video

    Each line types itself into a search box as it’s sung, because that’s where the year was written. No stock footage, no generated faces.
