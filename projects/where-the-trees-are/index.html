夏瑾禾
Xia Jinhe

Known online as 奇迹夏夏, Miracle Xiaxia. Born in Lujiang, Anhui, in November 2002. She lived with bone cancer for five years and died in Shanghai on 27 October 2026, aged 23.

This page keeps her story, in English and 中文, for anyone who wants to read it. My song for her, 有树的地方 (Where the Trees Are), is further down.

her story

Xia Jinhe grew up in Lujiang County, Anhui, raised by her grandmother. Online she was 奇迹夏夏, Miracle Xiaxia. At school in Hefei she studied art.

In 2021, at 18, she fainted from pain in her right leg in the middle of the gaokao maths paper. She came round and finished the paper. The pain was osteosarcoma, a bone cancer. Her offer from a teachers’ college reached her in a hospital ward. She never got to use it.

By her own account, she went through most of the treatment that followed without her parents. On Qixi, the Chinese Valentine’s Day, in August 2021, her right leg was amputated in Beijing. She signed the consent form herself. “A leg for a life,” she said. “Worth it.” Over five years she had six or seven major operations and 25 rounds of chemotherapy, in Beijing, Guangzhou and Shanghai. The cancer reached both lungs, then her ribs. She signed her own forms every time.

She saved for her own treatment and her prosthetic leg. When she was well enough, she went home and ran a street stall on that leg, selling bead bracelets and crocheted dolls she made herself. People offered to start a crowdfunding page for her. She said no: everyone works hard for their money.

Strangers who met her online became friends. A man in Zhejiang had her to stay and showed her round the province. A photo studio owner in Shantou took a series of photos of her, and he and his mother helped her make a home there. In her farewell she wrote that she had crossed the sea, climbed Mount Tai, and seen the flowers of Lijiang. “I met so much love along the way,” she said.

Nurses in Shanghai remember her arriving alone, pulling a suitcase, smiling. Her profile gave her age as 100, the age she hoped to reach. She liked to say that once life has dragged you onto its pirate ship, you may as well be a happy pirate.

In September 2026 she made a will leaving what she had to her hospital’s bone tumour department, to help other patients. In a video that month she said it was the pain she wanted to end, not her life.

Her last wishes were small: to see the sea, and to buy her grandmother new clothes. In a message typed for after she was gone, she thanked everyone, said sorry for breaking the deal and for being a deserter, and asked people to understand her pain. “The world stays online,” she wrote. “I’m logging off first.” She asked to be buried where there are trees, with flowers and grass beside her.

She died in Shanghai on 27 October 2026, at 8:28 in the evening. She was 23. In the comments on her account, thousands of strangers left the same words: 夏夏，晚安. Goodnight, Xiaxia.

On 2 November her family, classmates, carers and friends from across China said goodbye to her at Longhua Funeral Home in Shanghai. She wore a long dress that people who followed her online had sent.

I read about her on 30 October and kept thinking about the empty chair beside her bed. I never knew her. The song is sung by a stranger, the way all those strangers said goodnight. It follows her life in order and ends where she asked to be: somewhere with trees.

她的故事

夏瑾禾在安徽庐江长大，是奶奶把她带大的。在网上，她叫“奇迹夏夏”。她在合肥读书，学的是美术。

2021年，18岁的她在高考数学考场上，因为右腿剧痛晕倒。醒来以后，她把卷子写完了。那份疼，是骨肉瘤。师范学校的录取通知书寄到了病房，她再也没能用上。

据她自己说，之后的治疗，大多是她一个人面对的。2021年七夕，她在北京做了右腿截肢手术，同意书是她自己签的。“一条腿换一条命，很值。”五年里，她在北京、广州、上海辗转治疗，做了六七次大手术、二十五轮化疗。癌细胞转移到双肺，后来又到了肋骨。每一次，都是她自己签字。

她自己攒钱治病、装假肢。身体好一点，就回到家乡，装着假肢摆摊，卖自己串的珠子和钩的娃娃。有人提出替她发起筹款，她谢绝了：大家赚钱都不容易。

在网上认识的陌生人，后来成了她的朋友。浙江的许先生请她到家里住，带她在浙江各地走走。汕头一家影楼的沈先生为她拍了一组照片，和母亲一起帮她在汕头安了家。她在告别的话里写道：“我走过大海，越过泰山，见过云南丽江的花。”她说：“这一路遇到好多的爱。”

上海的护士记得，她第一次来的时候，一个人拖着行李箱，笑着走进来。她把主页上的年龄填成一百岁，那是她想活到的年纪。她常说：“既然上了生活的贼船，何不做一个快乐的海盗呢。”

2026年9月，她立下遗嘱，把自己的财产留给医院的骨肿瘤科，帮助其他病人。那个月，她在视频里说：“我想结束的是痛苦，而不是生命。”

她最后的心愿很小：想去看海，想给奶奶买新衣服。在提前写好的告别信里，她谢谢所有人，为“违约”道歉，说自己“当了逃兵”，请大家理解她的痛。“这个世界online，我先下线了哦。”她希望自己葬在有树木的地方，旁边有花草相伴。

2026年10月27日晚上8点28分，她在上海离世，年仅23岁。在她账号的评论区里，成千上万的陌生人留下同一句话：夏夏，晚安。

11月2日，家人、同学、护工和从各地赶来的朋友，在上海龙华殡仪馆送她最后一程。她穿着网友寄来的长裙。

10月30日，我读到她的故事，一直放不下她病床边那把空椅子。我不认识她。这首歌由一个陌生人来唱，就像那些对她说晚安的陌生人一样。歌按她走过的路一段段唱下去，最后停在她想去的地方：有树的地方。
