Photos of her
她的照片

Where to see photos of Xia Jinhe (夏瑾禾), Miracle Xiaxia, as her friends and the press first shared them.

The photos belong to her friends, her family and the reporters who took them, so each one is linked to where it was first published rather than copied here. If the people who hold them agree, I will show them on this page, with their names.

  • 28 Oct 2026

    Her final photo

    In her last days, wearing an oxygen mask, she got her own memorial photo ready. It was posted with her obituary on her account.

    最后的日子里，她戴着氧气面罩，亲手准备好了自己的遗照，随讣告发布在她的账号上。

    Posted by her friend Mr Shen (杰西大叔) on her account, 奇迹夏夏. See it in 腾讯新闻, 28 Oct ↗ and 澎湃新闻, 29 Oct ↗.

  • 2025

    The photo series in Shantou

    She saw a photo series Mr Shen had made for someone else and asked him to take one of her. He runs a photo studio in Shantou, and he and his mother later helped her make a home there.

    她看到沈先生为别人拍的一组照片，便请他也为自己拍一组。沈先生在汕头开影楼，后来和母亲一起帮她在汕头安了家。

    Taken by Mr Shen (杰西大叔). Reported by 现代快报 (Modern Express), via 诗华日报, 29 Oct ↗.

  • 2021 to 2026

    Her own posts: the stall, the dolls, the prosthetic leg

    Her own photos from five years: after the amputation, at her street stall, with the bead bracelets and crocheted dolls she made.

    她自己五年来发布的照片：截肢以后、摆摊的时候、她串的手串和钩的娃娃。

    From her account, 奇迹夏夏. Screenshots appear in 腾讯新闻, 4 Nov ↗.

  • 14 Jan 2026

    At Ruijin Hospital, Shanghai

    The morning she went for a CT scan, after the cancer had spread to her ribs and pleura. She told the reporter she meant to get through this round too.

    肋骨和胸膜转移后，她去做CT检查的那个早上。她对记者说，这一关也要闯过去。

    大皖新闻 (Da Wan News), reporter 孙召军. See 大皖新闻 on 腾讯新闻, 14 Jan 2026 ↗.

  • 2 Nov 2026

    Flowers at her farewell

    Flowers and cards from across China outside the farewell hall at Longhua Funeral Home, Shanghai.

    上海龙华殡仪馆告别厅外，来自全国各地的鲜花和卡片。

    澎湃新闻 (The Paper), reporter 陈斯斯. See 腾讯新闻, 4 Nov ↗.
